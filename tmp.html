TRUSTED GALVANIZED
LASTING VALUE

Tritama Mega Perkasa is a company engaged in the supply of galvanized steel materials for construction, fabrication, and industrial needs. Based in Kepulauan Riau, we provide galvanized products that are designed to offer strength, durability, and better resistance for various applications.
With a focus on quality and dependable service, Tritama Mega Perkasa supports customers who require materials for structural, roofing, partition, and general fabrication purposes. We are committed to providing strong materials, consistent availability, and professional support for every project.
