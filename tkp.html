SOLID FOUNDATION

Tritama Karya Prakarsa is a company engaged in the supply of black steel materials for construction, fabrication, manufacturing, and industrial needs. Based in Kepulauan Riau, we provide various steel products that support structural strength, production processes, and general project requirements.
We understand the importance of strong and reliable materials in every project. Therefore, Tritama Karya Prakarsa is committed to supplying quality black steel products, supported by professional service, dependable delivery, and long-term partnership values.
