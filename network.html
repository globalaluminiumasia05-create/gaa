Group Architecture

HOW THE GROUP IS STRUCTURED

Global Aluminium Asia operates as a holding group with 12 specialised branch companies, each independently managed but sharing centralised procurement, quality control, and logistics infrastructure.

This structure allows each branch to move quickly and serve its specific market segment — while benefiting from the Group's collective buying power, inventory depth, and nationwide distribution network.

Clients can access any branch independently or engage with our central sales team for multi-branch, multi-province supply arrangements.
