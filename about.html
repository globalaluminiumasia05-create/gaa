Company Background

BUILDING TRUST
ACROSS INDONESIA

Established in 2016, PT Global Aluminium Asia is an aluminium trading company dedicated to delivering high-quality products and professional services. As part of Golden Group, we continue expanding our presence across Indonesia to provide accessible, reliable, and innovative solutions for our customers.

Driven by sustainable growth and strong partnerships, we continuously adapt to evolving market needs while upholding excellence, responsibility, and customer satisfaction in every aspect of our business.
