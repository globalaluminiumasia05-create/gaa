COMPLETE SOLUTIONS

Global Prima Internusa is a company engaged in the supply of aluminium profiles, aluminium composite panels, and building accessories. Based in Kepulauan Riau, we provide complete material solutions to support construction, façade, interior, and fabrication projects.
We are committed to delivering products that combine strength, function, and modern design. Through a wide product range and reliable service, Global Prima Internusa supports fabricators, contractors, developers, and business partners in fulfilling their project requirements efficiently and professionally.
