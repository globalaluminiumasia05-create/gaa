Global Infinity Tritama is a Pekanbaru-based company engaged in the supply of aluminium extrusion, aluminium composite panel, and accessories. We provide integrated material solutions to support construction, architectural, façade, interior, and aluminium fabrication needs.
With a complete product range and strong service commitment, Global Infinity Tritama aims to help customers fulfill project requirements efficiently and professionally. We are dedicated to providing quality materials, reliable delivery, and dependable support for residential, commercial, and industrial sectors.
